---
title: "Managing lenses"
description: "Upload new versions, change the current version, and choose who can find a lens"
---

After you create a lens, you can change its details, upload new versions, roll back, and decide
whether it is listed in the marketplace. You manage lenses through
[Beacon Cloud MCP](/cloud/lenses/lenses-mcp): ask your agent, and it calls the tools for you.

Only the lens's owner can change it.

## Change details

The title, description and icon can change at any time. Ask your agent, for example:

```text
Rename my files-changed-plus lens to "Files Changed+" and update its description.
```

The key is permanent. To use a different key, create a new lens.

## Upload a new version

Every upload is a new, immutable version with its own semantic version number, such as `1.0.1`
or `1.1.0`. A version number can be used only once, so to change the HTML, upload another version.

Uploading a version does not change what anyone sees. It stays a draft until you release it.

## Release or roll back

Releasing a version makes it the **current version**: the one the lens page, the marketplace and
everyone who added the lens see. Releasing takes effect immediately.

To roll back, release an earlier version. Nothing is re-uploaded, and every version stays
available to release again.

## Who can see a lens

| Visibility | Who can see and add it |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | Only you. New lenses are private unless you choose otherwise. |
| **Public** | Everyone. The lens is listed in the marketplace once it has a released version. |

Change visibility by asking your agent, for example "Make my token-heatmap lens public."

Making a public lens private removes it from the marketplace straight away, and it disappears from
the sessions of everyone else who added it. You keep it.

## Your lenses

Ask your agent to list your lenses. It shows every lens you own, public or private, with its
current version. You can also list the lenses you added, or browse the marketplace, the same way.

Lenses cannot be deleted yet. To retire one, make it private.
